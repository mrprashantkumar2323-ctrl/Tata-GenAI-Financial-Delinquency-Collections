Developed as part of the **Tata GenAI-Powered Data Analytics Job Simulation** on Forage. This repository implements the **Geldium AI Collections Strategy** — uniting predictive delinquency modeling, autonomous agentic outreach loops, and human-in-the-loop governance with Responsible AI guardrails.

---

## 📌 Architecture Highlights
1. **Predictive Risk Scoring**: Gradient Boosting & Random Forest classifiers categorizing borrowers by default probability.
2. **Autonomous Agentic Decision Loops**: Multi-tier intervention system routing accounts between automated SMS, dynamic WhatsApp restructuring offers, and human hardship counseling.
3. **Responsible AI Guardrails**: SHAP-based model interpretability ensuring ECOA and GDPR compliance to eliminate demographic lending biases.
4. **Target Business KPI**: 10% delinquency rate reduction and improved borrower retention.
